# Support

Side Feather is a free, open-source public beta. Feedback and reproducible bug reports are welcome; response times and future releases are not guaranteed.

- [Installation and controls](README.md)
- [Report a bug or request a feature](https://github.com/mathewharwich/side-feather/issues)
- [Privacy policy](PRIVACY.md)
- [Report a security issue](SECURITY.md)
- Email: [support@dev.mathewharwich.com](mailto:support@dev.mathewharwich.com)

For a bug report, include your macOS version, Premiere version, Intel or Apple silicon, source/sequence dimensions, affected controls and steps to reproduce it. A small synthetic example is often enough. Share screenshots or media only if you are comfortable making them public.

The first release was tested in Premiere Pro 26.5.2 on an Intel Mac. The download contains Intel and Apple silicon code. Apple silicon runtime behavior, other Premiere versions, After Effects, Windows, interlaced media and HDR delivery have not been qualified.

Side Feather renders on the CPU. Several stacked 4K clips may need a lower playback resolution or rendered previews. If a previously saved project reports the effect missing, reinstall Side Feather and restart Premiere; removing the plug-in does not bake its result into existing projects.
