# Side Feather privacy policy

Effective October 30, 2026.

Side Feather is a local video effect for Adobe Premiere Pro, developed by Mathew Harwich.

## Your video stays in Premiere

The plug-in processes image buffers supplied by Premiere inside Premiere's process. It does not upload your video, images, audio, project files or effect settings. It has no account system, network service, advertising, analytics, usage tracking or telemetry.

Side Feather's controls are saved by Premiere as part of your project, using Premiere's normal effect settings. The plug-in does not maintain a separate project database or media cache.

## Installation and removal

The Mac installer places the plug-in in your account's Adobe MediaCore folder. When it replaces or removes a previous Side Feather installation, it preserves that plug-in in your account's Side Feather Backups folder. These local backups contain plug-in files, not your video or project media. The installer does not upload installation information or change your Adobe account.

Premiere and macOS may create their own diagnostic logs. Their behavior is separate from Side Feather and is covered by their respective policies.

## Downloads and support

The project website and GitHub downloads are separate from the installed plug-in. Their hosting services have their own privacy policies. Opening a support link or downloading a release uses those services; the plug-in does not contact them in the background.

If you choose to email support or open a GitHub issue, the information you submit is handled through that service and used to respond to your request. GitHub issues are public. Do not include private media, credentials or confidential project information in a public issue.

## Contact and changes

For privacy questions, contact [support@dev.mathewharwich.com](mailto:support@dev.mathewharwich.com).

Changes to this policy will be published with an updated effective date. The source code is available under the MIT license at [github.com/mathewharwich/side-feather](https://github.com/mathewharwich/side-feather).
